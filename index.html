Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our services, outlets, and turnaround procedures.

What services does Hangers provide? expand_more
Professional laundry, specialized dry cleaning, steam ironing, premium garment care, stain treatment, and pickup & delivery.
Where are Hangers outlets located? expand_more
We operate across 8 convenient outlets in East Delhi and Laxmi Nagar, New Delhi. You can click "Get Directions" on any outlet above to view the exact location in Google Maps.
Can I book a pickup? expand_more
Yes, you can schedule a pickup online through our booking panel or drop off garments directly at your nearest outlet.
Do you provide dry cleaning? expand_more
Yes, we provide specialized dry cleaning processes designed for tailored garments, silks, woolens, and delicate textiles.
Can I drop clothes directly at an outlet? expand_more
Yes, garments can be handed over directly at any of our 8 outlets throughout operating hours.
How do I find my nearest outlet? expand_more
Use our ‘Our Outlets’ section above to find your closest outlet and get instant directions via Google Maps.
How is the price determined? expand_more
Pricing is determined by garment type, fabric complexity, and the specific cleaning or finishing service required. Items are inspected on arrival before final invoicing.
How can I contact Hangers? expand_more
You can submit a pickup request or inquiry through our website, or visit any of our 8 neighborhood outlets across East Delhi.
